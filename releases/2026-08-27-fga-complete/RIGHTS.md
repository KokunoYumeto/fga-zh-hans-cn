# Rights, attribution, and non-endorsement

Historical author: Alexandre Grothendieck.

Primary evidence: the seventeen linked NUMDAM witnesses for the eight FGA
exposés, the 1962 *Commentaires*, and the eight errata. The scans are not
redistributed in this packet. The corrected French transcription and validated
English comparison edition are also excluded; only their identities and hashes
are retained as provenance.

This is an independent scholarly translation and typesetting project. It is not
an official edition and does not imply endorsement by the historical author,
the Séminaire Bourbaki, NUMDAM, a publisher, or any institution. No new blanket
public-domain or open-license claim is made over the underlying FGA text. The
Zenodo licence field must remain `notspecified`. Any downstream distribution
must preserve these provenance and rights limitations.
