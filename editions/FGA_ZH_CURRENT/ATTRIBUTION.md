# Sources and authorship

The original work is Alexandre Grothendieck’s *Fondements de la géométrie algébrique*: Bourbaki exposés 149, 182, 190, 195, 212, 221, 232 and 236, their errata, and the 1962 comments. [French originals](https://www.numdam.org/).

The Chinese translation is AI-translated; subsequent corrections, editing and typesetting are AI-produced. The publisher did not translate the text. The French text is the primary source; the maintained [English edition](https://github.com/KokunoYumeto/fga-en) is a secondary reference. That English reference contains wording shared with [Tim Hosgood’s human translation](https://github.com/thosgood/fga/tree/9976dd5f8e2ddcb388f0a07f3aa334f120d1019f), whose human authorship remains credited. This credits the English reference; it does not attribute the Chinese translation to Tim Hosgood. The identified upstream version is 9976dd5f8e2ddcb388f0a07f3aa334f120d1019f. The upstream translator also credits Steve Hnizdur for corrections and Jon Sterling for technical support. Those contributions concern the English reference, not the Chinese AI translation or its separate AI-produced corrections. Attribution does not establish permission or endorsement.
